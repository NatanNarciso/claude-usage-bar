## [0.1.0]
### Added
- Menu-bar mascot gauge (tank drains as usage grows) with live `%`
- Popover: gradient ring, time to reset, weekly limit, pace forecast
- Four animated mascot moods (calm, busy, sweating, asleep)
- Notifications at 80% / 95%, launch at login, manual refresh
- Menu-bar alert: shakes at 80%+, pulses at 90%+ (respects Reduce Motion)
- Clickable mascot with random reactions; party every 10th click
- "Limit is back!" celebration and notification when the 5-hour window renews
- English and Portuguese localization
- Docs for the undocumented usage endpoint; scripts to regenerate GIFs and screenshots
